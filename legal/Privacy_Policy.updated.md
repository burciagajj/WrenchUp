# WrenchUp Privacy Policy (Updated Draft)

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect account information (email, profile data), vehicle details, booking/job records, support messages, and payment method metadata required to operate the platform.

## 2. Location Data Usage
We collect and process precise and approximate location data to:
- match customers with nearby mechanics,
- calculate ETA and trip distance,
- power live tracking during active service flows,
- support safety, fraud prevention, and dispute review.

Location data may be captured while the app is in use and associated with job records.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use trusted third parties, including:
- Stripe for payment processing,
- mapping/location providers for routing and ETA,
- cloud infrastructure and storage providers,
- analytics and error-monitoring services.

These providers process personal data under their own privacy and security terms.

## 4. Data Retention
We retain:
- account/profile data while the account remains active,
- transaction, payment, and job records as required for legal, accounting, fraud, and support purposes,
- limited operational logs for security and reliability.

Retention periods vary by data category and legal obligations.

## 5. User Rights and Choices
Users can edit profile and vehicle information in-app and may request deletion of their account, subject to legal and compliance retention requirements.
